Cherry to kettle

Nine days in the life of your next bag.

Most coffee on Vietnamese shelves was roasted months ago. Ours is roasted to order on two fixed days, so the timeline from rested green bean to your door is short and written on the label.

  1. Day 1

    Green beans leave the farm

    Rested for at least 60 days after drying, then sorted by hand a second time at our table in Phường 8.

  2. Day 3

    Roasted in 6 kg batches

    Light-to-medium on a drum roaster, logged per batch. Every batch is cupped the next morning.

  3. Day 5

    Bagged and sent

    Valve bags, roast date stamped, packed in recycled paper. Couriered to HCMC and Hanoi.

  4. Day 7–9

    On your scale

    Best from day 7 to day 30 after roast. The recipe card in the bag starts you at 15 g to 250 ml.
